30-second UGC script

Problem → personal experience → product → relevant features → soft CTA.

HOOK: “I was honestly tired of charging my earbuds every single day.”

PROBLEM: “Between college, travelling and listening to music all day, my old earbuds were constantly running out of battery.”

PRODUCT: “Then I started using the boAt Nirvana Ion.”

EXPERIENCE: “The thing I noticed first was the battery. You get up to 120 hours of total playback, so charging isn't something I'm constantly thinking about.”

FEATURES: “They also have BEAST Mode with 60ms low latency for gaming, and quad-mic ENx technology for calls.”

CTA: “If you're looking for a pair of everyday earbuds, these are definitely worth checking out.”
