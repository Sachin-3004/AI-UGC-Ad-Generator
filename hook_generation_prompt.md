# UGC Hook Generation Prompt

Act as an experienced UGC advertising copywriter.

Create 10 short hooks for a UGC-style advertisement for:

Brand: boAt
Product: Nirvana Ion Wireless Earbuds

Target audience:
College students, gamers, music listeners and young professionals.

The hooks should:

- Sound like something a real person would say
- Feel natural and conversational
- Create curiosity within the first 3 seconds
- Avoid sounding like a traditional advertisement
- Focus on real customer problems
- Avoid exaggerated claims
- Use simple Indian-English language

Create hooks using different angles:

1. Problem-based
2. Curiosity-based
3. Personal experience
4. Recommendation
5. Before vs after
6. Student lifestyle
7. Gaming
8. Music
9. Battery
10. Calling

Keep each hook under 15 words.
